\caption{\textbf{The research gap as the intersection of three deficits.}
Resilience research is held back by three interlocking deficits---conceptual
plurality without operational convergence, limited empirical validation of
adaptive performance, and methodological fragmentation across domains. Each is
individually surmountable; their \emph{intersection} is what this review
targets: \emph{enacted adaptive performance}---resilience that can be observed
and evaluated rather than merely declared---remains out of reach because the
field simultaneously lacks a shared construct, cumulative evidence, and
transferable measures. The Enactment--Scope framework and the agenda of
Section~\ref{sec:RD} are organized to close that overlap.}
