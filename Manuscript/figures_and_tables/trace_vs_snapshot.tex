audit, organizations following very different trajectories are
indistinguishable---a single snapshot cannot separate resilience from luck or
compliance from capability. Only by tracing adaptive capacity across successive
disruptions does it become observable whether an organization grows stronger,
returns unchanged, or quietly becomes more brittle.}
